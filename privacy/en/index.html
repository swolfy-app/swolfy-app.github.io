1. Summary

  • Your swims, heart rate and profile are stored only on your device and are never sent to the Company. The Company runs no servers.
  • The App shows Google AdMob ads. Google may collect your advertising ID and other device information to serve ads. Swim records, heart rate and other health information are never used for or shared with advertising.
  • The only personal information the Company receives is the email you choose to send when reporting a problem.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your device is deleted as soon as you delete a swim, clear the App's data or uninstall the App. The Company keeps no copy.
  • Support emails are kept for one year after we answer and then deleted, unless a law requires a longer period.
  • Electronic files are deleted so they cannot be recovered.

6. Backups

  • Android automatic backup: If Google backup is on, Android backs up the App's swims and settings, encrypted, to your Google account and restores them on a new phone. Google keeps this backup in your account; the Company cannot access it. You can turn it off in your phone settings.
  • Backup file export: Only when you tap "Export backup file", the App saves a file with your swims (including heart rate), pools and profile to a location you choose. You decide where it is kept and who it is shared with; the App never sends it anywhere.

7. Automatic collection tools

The App uses no cookies. The Google Mobile Ads SDK uses your advertising ID for ads. You can delete or reset it in Settings → Google → Ads; once deleted, you will not see personalised ads.

8. Your rights

  • You can view, edit and delete your swims and profile in the App at any time.
  • You can ask us by email to access, correct, delete or stop processing the information we hold (your support messages), and we will act without delay.
  • Children under 14 should contact us with a parent's or guardian's consent. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

9. Security

  • Swim and health data are never gathered on a server, which removes the risk of a bulk leak.
  • App data is kept in Android's app-private storage that other apps cannot read.
  • Access to support emails is kept to a minimum.
